<p class="lead">You are ready to use Helm for a small project when you can complete these tasks without copying each earlier command.</p>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="h-g-explain"><span>Explain chart, values, release, and revision—and what Kubernetes does after Helm finishes.</span></label>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="h-g-render"><span>Change a value, preview the resulting manifest, and identify the field that changed.</span></label>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="h-g-run"><span>Install your chart in a learning namespace and reach its Service from your browser.</span></label>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="h-g-recover"><span>Diagnose a failed upgrade and roll back to a known successful revision.</span></label>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="h-g-own"><span>Keep the chart and non-secret values in git, then uninstall the practice release and check what remains.</span></label>
<p><b>Next project:</b> package your own small app, document its values, and practice upgrading it in a test environment. Revisit Kubernetes Day 2 for monitoring, storage, backups, and cluster upgrades; Helm does not replace those responsibilities.</p>
