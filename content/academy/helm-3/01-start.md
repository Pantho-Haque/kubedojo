<p class="lead">You know how Kubernetes keeps an app running. Helm helps you package the instructions and use them again with different settings.</p>
<div class="box idea"><div class="tag">The big idea</div>Think of a chart as a recipe book, values as your choices, and a release as one named installation made from that recipe.</div>
<p><b>Before starting:</b> understand a Deployment, a Service, and YAML from Kubernetes Stages 1–2. Use the topic dropdown to revisit them. No installation is needed until the final stage here.</p>
