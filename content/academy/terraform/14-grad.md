<p class="lead">You are ready to use Terraform on a small project when you can complete these tasks without copying earlier commands.</p>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="tf-g-explain"><span>Explain resource, provider, state, and plan, and where Terraform fits next to Docker, Kubernetes, and Helm.</span></label>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="tf-g-loop"><span>Write a small configuration and run init → plan → apply, reading the plan before approving it.</span></label>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="tf-g-read"><span>Point at a plan line and say whether it creates, updates in place, replaces, or destroys, and why.</span></label>
<label class="task"><input type="checkbox" data-task="tf-g-drift"><span>Cause drift, find it with plan, and choose whether to undo it or update the code.</span></label>
