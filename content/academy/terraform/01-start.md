<p class="lead">You can package an app (Docker), run it (Kubernetes), and install it with one command (Helm). But where do the servers, networks, and the cluster itself come from? Terraform builds them from files you write.</p>
<div class="box idea"><div class="tag">The big idea</div>Write down the infrastructure you want. Terraform compares it with what exists, shows you a plan, and makes only the changes you approve.</div>
<p><b>Before starting:</b> nothing is required. Knowing what a server and a network are helps. No installation or cloud account is needed until the final stage, and even then everything runs on your own computer for free.</p>
